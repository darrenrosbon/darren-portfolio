What I build

Made for small business

Quote and invoice systems

Type in the details. The system does the rest.

  • Add the client and items, and the quote or invoice builds itself
  • Numbered, named and saved automatically, with no starting from scratch
  • Sent to your client on WhatsApp or email in one tap

Lead tracking systems

Every enquiry in one place, with the next step always clear.

  • Leads from your site, ads and WhatsApp in one list, with their phone, website and notes
  • Mark each one new, contacted, replied or done, so nothing gets forgotten
  • A follow-up list of who hasn't answered after a few days, with a WhatsApp message ready to send
  • Message templates that fill in the lead's name for you, plus a simple dashboard of replies

Reminder and follow-up systems

Set it up once. It says the right thing at the right time.

  • Quote follow-ups, appointment reminders and review requests, sent to people who asked or are already customers
  • Messages spaced out over time or a time window, never all at once
  • Templates that fill in each person's name and details
  • Stops when someone replies, and remembers who you've messaged so nobody gets it twice

Online booking systems

Let customers book without the back and forth.

  • A booking page where customers pick a free time slot
  • Appointments land in your calendar, with confirmations sent for you
  • Fewer no-shows, with reminders sent automatically

Shared inbox systems

All your customer messages in one place.

  • Website chat, email and WhatsApp messages arrive in one inbox
  • Hand conversations to the right person and leave private notes
  • See the full history with every customer
  • Pairs with a chatbot that answers first, and a person who takes over

Automation systems

Your apps, working together without you.

  • When something happens in one app, the next step runs by itself
  • For example, a filled-in form adds a row to a sheet and sends a WhatsApp message
  • Connects the tools you already use, with no more copying and pasting

Job cards and scheduling

For teams that work out in the field.

  • Jobs, addresses and who is going where
  • Photos and notes from site
  • Works on a phone, on any data bundle

Monitoring and reporting

Know how your site is doing before anyone tells you.

  • An alert the moment your site stops working, so it gets fixed fast
  • Visitor numbers and where they came from, without cookies or tracking individuals
  • A plain monthly report of what your site and ads brought in
